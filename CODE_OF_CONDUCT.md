# Code of Conduct

## My commitment

I want this project to support open, factual, and respectful collaboration,
regardless of experience, origin, identity, ability, or personal background.

I value constructive feedback, reproducible technical arguments, patience with
different experience levels, and ownership of mistakes. I do not tolerate
harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, publication of private
information, or personal attacks.

## Enforcement

I may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behaviour
violates this code. I ask people to report sensitive incidents through the
private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), without placing personal details
in a public issue.

I based this concise policy on the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
